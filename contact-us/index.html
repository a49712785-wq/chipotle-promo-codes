Contact Us

Found a code that doesn't work, a program detail that's changed, or just have a question? Here's how to reach us.

Email

The fastest way to reach us is by email: contact@chipotlecoupons.us

We read every message. We aim to respond within 2–3 business days, though corrections about an expired or incorrect code are usually prioritized and handled faster.

What to Contact Us About

  • Corrections. If a code, price, or program detail on the site is outdated or wrong, tell us — ideally with a link or screenshot of the current, correct information.
  • General questions. If something on a page is unclear, ask — it usually means the page needs to explain it better, and we'd rather fix that.
  • Business and partnership inquiries. For advertising, partnership, or press inquiries, use the same email and include a short description of what you're proposing.

This Isn't Chipotle Customer Service

Chipotle Savings Guide is an independent site and can't access your order, your Rewards account, or your payment details, and we can't issue refunds or resolve in-restaurant issues. For order problems, Rewards account issues, or anything tied to your specific account, contact Chipotle directly through the app, chipotle.com, or their customer service channels.
