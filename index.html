Before You Default to Base Points: Check These First

Base Rewards points (10 per $1) are the baseline Rewards earning rate, not an instant discount at checkout. They're credit toward a future free entree. Three other current mechanisms can beat that baseline in immediate value on a given order, all of them personalized or rotating:

  • Your Offers tab. Chipotle sends account-specific offers that don't appear in any public list.
  • This Monday's Rewards Exchange promotion. Chipotle features a new weekly promotion in the Rewards Exchange every Monday; check what's currently live before assuming base points are your only option.
  • Any active Side Quest. Side Quests are defined per participant in-app and can offer bonus points beyond the base rate.

If none of those apply to your order, base points are your fallback, not your first answer.

Chipotle Rewards: The Actual Numbers

You earn 10 points per $1 spent (tax and tip excluded) as the baseline Rewards earning rate. 1,625 points redeems a free entree. A $59-70 Build-Your-Own Chipotle order earns 590-700 points at once—roughly 36-43% of the way to a free entree in one purchase. A typical $20 individual order earns 200 points, about 12% of the way. Points stay active as long as you make one qualifying purchase a year.

Direct Ordering vs. DoorDash, Uber Eats, or Grubhub

Ordering through chipotle.com or the Chipotle app keeps you eligible for TRYBYOC, SUNDAYS (if applicable), and Rewards points on the full order. Order the same thing through a third-party platform, and none of that applies—Chipotle doesn't track those orders for Rewards at all.

A $59 group order that would've earned 590 points earns zero through a delivery app. This is a real comparison: if a delivery app's own current promotion is large enough to outweigh the points and program eligibility you'd give up, it can still be the better choice for that specific order. When no platform-specific deal is running, ordering direct usually comes out ahead.

Is a Code You Found Elsewhere Actually Real?

Beyond the offers above, other Chipotle codes do circulate, but the ones that check out are short-lived, typically active for just 2 to 5 days around a specific event. Before trying one:

  • Does it read like a dated campaign? Some legitimate event codes use recognizable formats, such as a food abbreviation plus a year (TRYBYOC and TAGTEAM both followed this pattern), though format alone doesn't confirm authenticity.
  • Does it show up on more than one source with matching terms? A real code comes with full restrictions attached, matching across sources.
  • Is it listed on an official Chipotle channel? Check chipotle.com/offers, the official app, or Chipotle's press releases before relying on a third-party coupon site.

In the sources checked for this page, sites listing a large number of "active" codes at once tended to include several that were expired or didn't match any verifiable pattern. TRYBYOC's own history is a useful example: an expired 2025 version (different dates, different redemption cap) is still shown as current on some sites.

If Something Doesn't Apply at Checkout

  • Not logged into Rewards. True for nearly every offer on this page.
  • Trying to combine offers. TRYBYOC allows one discount per transaction and can't be combined with other coupons or promotions.
  • A window closed or a cap was hit. TRYBYOC's 100,000-redemption cap, a dated event code's few-day window, or SUNDAYS on a non-participating Sunday.
  • Wrong channel. TRYBYOC and most current codes require chipotle.com or the app, not in-restaurant, not catering (except standing catering pricing), not third-party delivery.
  • Off hours or wrong day. SUNDAYS requires after 3 PM on a select Sunday; Rewards Exchange rotates every Monday.

Bottom Line

  • Solo order: Check your Offers tab, this week's Rewards Exchange promotion, and any active Side Quest before defaulting to baseline Rewards points.
  • Group of 4-6: Compare TRYBYOC on a Build-Your-Own Chipotle order against ordering individual meals for your group's size.
  • Ordering on a Sunday after 3 PM: Check if SUNDAYS is running that week; if so, buying 2+ entrees gives you a free one.
  • Considering third-party delivery: Weigh the platform's actual current promotion against the Rewards points and program eligibility you'd give up by not ordering direct.
  • Found a random coupon code: Verify it against chipotle.com before relying on it at checkout.
