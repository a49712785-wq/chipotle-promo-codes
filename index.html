How to Redeem a Chipotle Promo Code

  1. Build your order on chipotle.com or in the Chipotle app, then open your bag.
  2. Find "Enter a Promo Code." On the web it sits in your bag, under "Sign in to use rewards." Type or paste the code and tap Apply.
  3. Check the subtotal before you pay. The discount appears right away — if it doesn't, the code hasn't applied. One code per order; codes can't be combined with other offers.

Ordering in the app? Some Chipotle offers live in your Rewards Wallet instead of the promo box: tap your profile, go to My Rewards → "Add an Offer Code," save the offer, then select it at checkout. Personalized offers (your Offers tab, Side Quests) only appear when you're logged in.

Before You Default to Base Points: Check These First

Base Rewards points (10 per $1) are the baseline Rewards earning rate, not an instant discount at checkout, they're credit toward a future free entree. Three other current mechanisms can beat that baseline in immediate value on a given order, all of them personalized or rotating, so this page can tell you they exist but not what's currently in yours:

  • Your Offers tab. Chipotle sends account-specific offers that don't appear in any public list.
  • This Monday's Rewards Exchange promotion. Chipotle features a new weekly promotion in the Rewards Exchange every Monday, check what's currently live before assuming base points are your only option.
  • Any active Side Quest. Chipotle sometimes assigns Side Quests defined per participant in-app; these aren't publicly documented and can offer bonus points beyond the base rate.

If none of those apply to your order, base points are your fallback, not your first answer, and they're a long-term earning rate rather than a same-day discount.

Group Orders: The Real Math Without a Code

Build-Your-Own Chipotle is one specific product: a digital-exclusive meal kit launched in 2025 that feeds 4-6 people, priced roughly $59 (chicken or Sofritas) to $70 (braised beef barbacoa), ready in about 15 minutes for pickup or available for delivery at a higher price. It does not apply to a regular individual bowl or burrito.

Group Size Full Price Range Per-Person Cost

6 people $59-70 $9.83-11.67

Chipotle doesn't publish a single national average for an individual bowl, actual price depends on your protein, add-ons, and location. For a rough comparison point: multiple 2026 menu-price trackers put a chicken bowl in the $9.75-$10.45 range nationally, with guacamole adding roughly $2.65-$2.95, putting a chicken bowl with guac in the neighborhood of $12.50-$13.50 in most U.S. markets (these are third-party aggregated estimates, not Chipotle-published figures, and your local price could run higher or lower).

The verdict, by group size (at full price, no code):

  • 6 people: a Build-Your-Own Chipotle order comes out cheaper per person than separate individual bowls
  • 5 people: roughly a wash, depends on protein and what each person would add individually
  • 4 people: more expensive per person than individual bowls, unless sharing suits the group

The portions are family-style rather than a guaranteed individual bowl each, so this favors groups fine with sharing rather than everyone wanting a specific, separate order.

Chipotle Rewards: The Actual Numbers

You earn 10 points per $1 spent (tax and tip excluded) as the baseline Rewards earning rate, and 1,625 points redeems a free entree, a rate confirmed on Chipotle's own rewards page. A $59-70 Build-Your-Own Chipotle order earns 590-700 points at once, roughly 36-43% of the way to a free entree in one purchase. A typical $20 individual order earns 200 points, about 12% of the way there. This is future redemption value, not an immediate percentage off your bill, the discount only materializes once you actually redeem the points. Points stay active as long as you make one qualifying purchase a year, and they apply to every order, not one specific product.

Direct Ordering vs. DoorDash, Uber Eats, or Grubhub

Ordering through chipotle.com or the Chipotle app keeps you eligible for Rewards points on the full order. Through a third-party platform, Chipotle does not track those orders for Rewards at all — a $59 group order that would earn 590 points direct earns zero through a delivery app, on top of the platform's own fees.

This is a comparison, not an automatic answer: if a delivery app's own current promotion is large enough to outweigh the points and program eligibility you would give up, it can still win for that specific order. When no platform deal is running, ordering direct usually comes out ahead, because of what it preserves.

Is a Code You Found Elsewhere Actually Real?

Beyond the offers above, other Chipotle codes do circulate, but the ones that check out are short-lived, typically active for just 2 to 5 days around a specific event like National Avocado Day or National Quesadilla Day. Before trying one:

  • Does it read like a dated campaign, not a random string? Some legitimate event codes use recognizable campaign or event-related formats, such as a food abbreviation plus a year, though format alone doesn't confirm authenticity, plenty of expired or fabricated codes look the same way.
  • Does it show up on more than one source with matching terms? A real code comes with full restrictions attached, matching across sources.
  • Does it require a Rewards account? Many legitimate Chipotle offers require a Rewards account, though not universally, so this is a signal to weigh, not a pass/fail test on its own.

In the sources checked for this page, sites listing a large number of "active" codes at once tended to include several that were expired or didn't match any verifiable pattern, that's a pattern observed in this research, not a claim about every coupon site. Old versions of a code from a previous year are still shown as current on some sites, which is exactly the kind of entry worth double-checking against Chipotle's own terms directly.

If Something Doesn't Apply at Checkout

  • Not logged into Rewards. True for nearly every offer on this page.
  • Trying to combine offers. Most offers allow one discount per transaction and can't be combined with other coupons or promotions.
  • A window closed or a cap was hit. A dated event code's few-day window, or a redemption cap, can close an offer early.
  • Wrong channel. Most current codes require chipotle.com or the app, not in-restaurant, not catering (except standing catering pricing), not third-party delivery.

Where do I enter a Chipotle promo code?
