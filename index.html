Before You Default to Base Points: Check These First

Base Rewards points (10 per $1) are the baseline Rewards earning rate, not an instant discount at checkout, they're credit toward a future free entree. Three other current mechanisms can beat that baseline in immediate value on a given order, all of them personalized or rotating, so this page can tell you they exist but not what's currently in yours:

  • Your Offers tab. Chipotle sends account-specific offers that don't appear in any public list.
  • This Monday's Rewards Exchange promotion. Chipotle features a new weekly promotion in the Rewards Exchange every Monday, check what's currently live before assuming base points are your only option.
  • Any active Side Quest. Chipotle sometimes assigns Side Quests defined per participant in-app; these aren't publicly documented and can offer bonus points beyond the base rate.

If none of those apply to your order, base points are your fallback, not your first answer, and they're a long-term earning rate rather than a same-day discount.

Chipotle Rewards: The Actual Numbers

You earn 10 points per $1 spent (tax and tip excluded) as the baseline Rewards earning rate, and 1,625 points redeems a free entree, a rate confirmed on Chipotle's own rewards page. A $59-70 Build-Your-Own Chipotle order earns 590-700 points at once, roughly 36-43% of the way to a free entree in one purchase. A typical $20 individual order earns 200 points, about 12% of the way there. This is future redemption value, not an immediate percentage off your bill, the discount only materializes once you actually redeem the points. Points stay active as long as you make one qualifying purchase a year, and they apply to every order, not one specific product the way TRYBYOC does.

Direct Ordering vs. DoorDash, Uber Eats, or Grubhub

Ordering through chipotle.com or the Chipotle app keeps you eligible for TRYBYOC and Rewards points on the full order. Chipotle's own delivery is available too, typically at a higher menu price than pickup, but it's still their direct channel and still earns Rewards.

Order the same thing through a third-party platform, and none of that applies, Chipotle doesn't track those orders for Rewards at all. A $59 group order that would've earned 590 points earns zero through a delivery app, on top of whatever fee the platform itself charges.

This is a real comparison, not an automatic answer: if a delivery app's own current promotion is large enough to outweigh the points and program eligibility you'd give up, it can still be the better choice for that specific order. When no platform-specific deal is running, ordering direct usually comes out ahead, specifically because of what it preserves (points, program eligibility), not because delivery apps are inherently worse.

Is a Code You Found Elsewhere Actually Real?

Beyond the offers above, other Chipotle codes do circulate, but the ones that check out are short-lived, typically active for just 2 to 5 days around a specific event like National Avocado Day or National Quesadilla Day. Before trying one:

  • Does it read like a dated campaign, not a random string? Some legitimate event codes use recognizable campaign or event-related formats, such as a food abbreviation plus a year (the current TRYBYOC code follows this pattern), though format alone doesn't confirm authenticity, plenty of expired or fabricated codes look the same way.
  • Does it show up on more than one source with matching terms? A real code comes with full restrictions attached, matching across sources.
  • Does it require a Rewards account? Many legitimate Chipotle offers require a Rewards account, though not universally, so this is a signal to weigh, not a pass/fail test on its own.

In the sources checked for this page, sites listing a large number of "active" codes at once tended to include several that were expired or didn't match any verifiable pattern, that's a pattern observed in this research, not a claim about every coupon site. TRYBYOC's own history is a useful example: an expired 2025 version (different dates, different redemption cap) is still shown as current on some sites, which is exactly the kind of entry worth double-checking against chipotle.com/byoc-legal directly.

If Something Doesn't Apply at Checkout

  • Not logged into Rewards. True for nearly every offer on this page.
  • Trying to combine offers. TRYBYOC allows one discount per transaction and can't be combined with other coupons or promotions.
  • A window closed or a cap was hit. TRYBYOC's 100,000-redemption cap or a dated event code's few-day window.
  • Wrong channel. TRYBYOC and most current codes require chipotle.com or the app, not in-restaurant, not catering (except standing catering pricing), not third-party delivery.

Chipotle Promo Code FAQs

Does Chipotle have coupons?

Not a standing coupon program — there's no permanent coupon page or weekly circular. What Chipotle does run is occasional official codes (right now: TRYBYOC, $10 off a Build-Your-Own order through December 31, 2026) plus the year-round Rewards program. If a site lists dozens of "active" Chipotle coupons at once, treat the list as unverified.

Does Chipotle offer delivery promo codes?

There's no separate delivery-code program. The one live code, TRYBYOC, works on the Chipotle app and chipotle.com only — its official terms exclude third-party platforms like DoorDash, Uber Eats, and Grubhub. Ordering direct also sidesteps delivery markups, which is usually the bigger saving anyway (see the delivery comparison above).

Are there Chipotle Rewards codes?

Chipotle Rewards itself is points-based, not code-based — you earn 10 points per $1 and redeem from the Rewards Exchange. But members do get exclusive promo codes and surprise drops according to Chipotle's own Rewards page, plus a free bonus reward drop every month. No code needed to join.

Why do so many Chipotle codes online not work?

Because most coupon sites never verify what they list. We checked roughly 70 codes across the top-ranking pages: the large majority were expired, single-use, or never official to begin with. Chipotle has no public coupon program, so any "50% off sitewide" style code with no link to Chipotle's own terms is unverifiable by definition. We only list codes we can trace to an official source.
