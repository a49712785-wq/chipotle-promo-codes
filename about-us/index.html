About Chipotle Savings Guide

We built this site to answer one question honestly: out of everything claiming to save you money at Chipotle, what actually works right now? No recycled code lists, no guessing — every page is checked against Chipotle's own published terms before it's published.

What We Do

Chipotle Savings Guide is an independent, reader-supported resource covering Chipotle promo codes, Rewards mechanics, seasonal programs, and the student and military discount questions people actually search for. We don't list a code unless we can trace it to a source Chipotle itself stands behind — its app, its official terms pages, or its newsroom.

A lot of coupon sites list dozens of "active" codes at once, many of them expired or fabricated, because more codes look better than fewer. We do the opposite: if a discount isn't currently real, we say so directly, and we explain what does work instead.

How We Verify Information

  • Primary sources first. Program terms are checked against chipotle.com, the Chipotle app, and Chipotle's official newsroom announcements — not against other coupon sites.
  • Dated, not evergreen. Promotions expire. Every page states when it was last verified, and we revisit pages as programs change rather than leaving stale claims live.
  • We separate fact from possibility. Account-specific offers, personalized deals, and unverified third-party claims are clearly labeled as such, rather than presented as if they apply to everyone.
